# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Personas in Spain looking for practical explanations about everyday personal-finance questions.

> Inferred from the site's navigation, copy and article topics; not a user-supplied audience definition.

## Product Purpose

FinClaro explains personal-finance topics in Spanish and provides practical guides and calculators covering saving, mortgages, salary/taxes, investing, bank accounts and loans.

## Positioning

The site combines plain-language financial guides with calculators and timely Spain-specific explainers.

> Inferred from the current homepage structure and content.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from search with a specific financial question and need a fast path to an explanation, example or calculator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static web pages with HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- Preserve existing URLs, article content, SEO metadata, analytics and AdSense-related structure unless explicitly requested otherwise.
- Responsive web experience for desktop and mobile.
- Financial information should remain clear and should not imply personalized financial advice.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: FinClaro.
- Spanish-language, clear and practical voice.
- Trust-oriented editorial presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing homepage, global stylesheet and current article/tool structure in the repository.
- Existing SEO metadata and structured data.
- Existing calculator entry points.

## Product Principles

1. Make the answer easy to find.
2. Explain financial concepts without unnecessary jargon.
3. Put useful tools close to relevant explanations.
4. Preserve trust through clear, restrained presentation.
5. Do not trade SEO/content integrity for visual decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
